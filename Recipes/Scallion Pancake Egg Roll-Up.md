# Scallion Pancake Egg Roll-Up

A frozen scallion pancake crisped in a pan, wrapped around a soft scramble seasoned with soy sauce and hoisin. Jianbing energy with none of the work — about five minutes start to finish.

**Total Time:** 5-7 minutes
**Servings:** 1 roll-up

## Ingredients

- 1 frozen scallion pancake
- 1.5 large eggs, beaten
- 1 splash soy sauce (~1 tsp)
- 1 splash hoisin sauce (~1 tsp)
- Neutral oil, if your pancake isn't pre-oiled

## Instructions

1. Cook the frozen pancake first, straight from frozen — do not thaw it, or it goes limp. Dry nonstick pan over medium heat (or a slick of oil if the pancake isn't pre-oiled), 2-3 minutes per side until it's blistered, golden, and puffed. Slide it onto a plate and leave it there; it'll stay hot while you do the eggs.

2. Beat the eggs. Wipe the pan, add a small amount of oil, and set it back over medium-low.

3. Pour in the eggs and let them set into a thin flat sheet roughly the size of the pancake — nudge the edges in once or twice, but don't scramble them into curds. You want one loose sheet you can lift in a single piece.

4. Once the top is just barely wet, flip the sheet.

5. Immediately hit the cooked side with the splash of soy sauce and the splash of hoisin, and spread them across the surface. Doing this after the flip means the sauces sit on top rather than steaming into the pan and burning.

6. Slide the egg onto the pancake, sauced side up. Roll it up tightly and eat it in hand.
