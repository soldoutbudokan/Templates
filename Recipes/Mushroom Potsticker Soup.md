# Mushroom Potsticker Soup

Dark, mushroom-heavy broth, no heat. Crisp-bottomed potstickers go in at the bowl.

**Prep Time:** 20 minutes
**Cook Time:** 30 minutes
**Servings:** 6

## Ingredients

### The Broth

- 3 tbsp neutral oil
- 14 oz cremini or fresh shiitake mushrooms, sliced thick
- 9 garlic cloves, smashed and sliced
- 3 tbsp fresh ginger, minced
- 6 scallions, whites and greens separated
- 3 tbsp Shaoxing wine
- 12 cups homemade unsalted broth
- 2 tsp hondashi granules
- 1.5 tbsp soy sauce
- 0.3 tsp MSG
- 2 tsp sugar
- 0.8 tsp ground white pepper
- 4 cups napa cabbage, shredded

### The Finish

- 1.5 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1.5 tbsp toasted sesame oil

### The Potstickers

- 36 Siwin potstickers, frozen
- Neutral oil for the pan
- 1/3 cup water, for steaming

## Instructions

1. **Brown the mushrooms hard:** Heat the 3 tbsp neutral oil in a large pot over high. Add the mushrooms in one layer and leave them alone until the undersides are deep brown, then stir and brown the other side, about 6 minutes. Don't stir early. With no dried shiitake in the pot, this is where the depth comes from.

2. **Add the aromatics:** Drop the heat to medium. Add the garlic, ginger, and scallion whites. Stir constantly until fragrant and pale gold, about 1 minute 30 seconds.

3. **Build and simmer the broth:** Pour in the Shaoxing wine and scrape the pot. Add the broth, hondashi, soy sauce, MSG, sugar, and white pepper. Bring to a boil, then hold at a steady simmer for 12 minutes.

4. **Crisp the potstickers:** While the broth simmers, heat a film of oil in a nonstick skillet over medium-high. Lay the frozen potstickers flat-side down and fry undisturbed until deeply browned, 8 minutes. Add the 1/3 cup water, cover, and steam 5 minutes. Uncover and cook off the last of the water. Work in batches if 36 won't fit flat.

5. **Add the cabbage:** Stir the napa cabbage into the broth and simmer 2 minutes — it should still have bite. Taste the broth now, before any potstickers go near it.

6. **Finish off the heat:** Kill the heat. Stir in the rice vinegar and toasted sesame oil. Both lose their edge if they boil.

7. **Assemble:** Divide the potstickers among bowls, ladle broth and cabbage over, and top with the scallion greens.

## Notes

Salt: hondashi is down to 2 tsp and soy to 1.5 tbsp from the last run. Taste at the end of step 5 and add soy a teaspoon at a time if it needs it — easier than pulling salt back out. The potstickers keep seasoning the broth as they sit in it, so under-salt on purpose.

Without the dried shiitake, the fresh mushrooms are doing all the mushroom work, so there's 14 oz here instead of 12. Brown them in two batches if your pot is crowded — steamed grey mushrooms give you nothing.

Store broth and potstickers separately. Reheat the broth to a simmer, crisp the potstickers in a dry pan for 2 minutes, then combine. The mushrooms and cabbage both hold up.
