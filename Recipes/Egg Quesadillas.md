# Egg Quesadillas

A fried egg and a handful of cheese folded into a small tortilla and toasted until crispy. Two of these is a meal; each one takes about three minutes.

**Total Time:** 6-8 minutes for two
**Servings:** 2 quesadillas (scales linearly)

## Ingredients (per quesadilla)

- 1 small flour tortilla (6 inch)
- 1 large egg
- Shredded cheese (~2 tbsp / 15g)
- Pinch of salt
- Neutral oil for frying

## Instructions

1. Heat a slick of oil in a nonstick pan over medium heat. Crack in the egg, salt it, and fry until the white is set. Break the yolk and flatten the egg out a bit with your spatula so it lies flat inside the fold — a domed yolk keeps the tortilla from closing and the cheese from sealing.

2. Slide the egg out onto a plate. Wipe the pan if it's messy.

3. Lay the tortilla in the dry pan over medium heat. Scatter half the cheese over one half of it, lay the egg on top, then the rest of the cheese over the egg. Cheese on both sides is what glues the fold shut.

4. Once the cheese starts going glossy, fold the bare half over the top and press down with the spatula.

5. Toast 1-2 minutes per side, pressing occasionally, until both sides are crisp and browned in patches and the cheese has fully melted.

6. Slide out, let it sit 30 seconds so the cheese sets enough to not run out, then eat. Repeat for the second one.
