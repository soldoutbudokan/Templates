# Chicken Thighs with Sherry Mushroom Pan Sauce

Seared boneless thighs in a sherry, mushroom and Dijon pan sauce, with or without cream. No stock needed, and built to reheat well in the microwave. Serve with a tray of roasted vegetables.

**Prep Time:** 10 minutes (plus optional 30 minute rest)
**Cook Time:** 35 minutes
**Servings:** 4

## Ingredients

### The Chicken

- 900g boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 1¼ tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp neutral oil

### The Sauce

- 225g cremini mushrooms, sliced
- 1 large shallot, minced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp fresh thyme leaves (or ½ tsp dried)
- 2 tsp all-purpose flour
- ¾ cup (190 ml) dry sherry (fino or amontillado)
- 1 cup (240 ml) water
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- ⅓ cup (75 ml) heavy cream (35%), cream version only
- 2 tbsp cold unsalted butter
- 1 tsp lemon juice

## Instructions

1. **Season the chicken:** Pat the thighs dry with paper towels and season both sides with the salt and pepper. If you have time, let them sit at room temperature for 30 minutes. Skip this if you don't.

2. **Sear the chicken:** Heat the oil in a large stainless or cast-iron skillet over medium-high (not nonstick, which won't build fond). Add the thighs without crowding, working in batches if needed. Cook without moving for 5-10 minutes until deeply browned, flip, and cook 4-5 minutes more until they reach at least 74°C (165°F). Thighs stay juicy up to 80-85°C. Move to a plate.

3. **Brown the mushrooms:** Pour off all but about 1 tbsp of fat. Add the mushrooms with a pinch of salt and cook, stirring now and then, until browned and their liquid has cooked off, 5-6 minutes.

4. **Add aromatics:** Lower the heat to medium. Add the shallot, garlic and thyme and cook for 1½ minutes. Sprinkle in the flour and stir for 30 seconds.

5. **Deglaze with sherry:** Pour in the sherry and scrape up the browned bits from the bottom of the pan. Simmer until reduced by about half, 2-3 minutes.

6. **Simmer the sauce:** Whisk in the water and Dijon. Simmer until the sauce thickens enough to coat a spoon, 4-5 minutes.

7. **Add cream (cream version only):** Stir in the cream and simmer 1-2 minutes until the sauce coats a spoon again. For the version without cream, skip to the next step.

8. **Finish and serve:** Take the pan off the heat. Swirl in the cold butter until melted and glossy, then add the lemon juice. Taste and add salt if needed. Return the chicken and any juices from the plate, turn to coat, and serve.

## Storage and Reheating

1. **Cool before storing:** Let the portions you're saving cool on the counter for up to 30 minutes. Get them into the fridge within 2 hours of cooking.

2. **Portion and refrigerate:** Divide into airtight, microwave-safe containers, about 2 thighs per container, and spoon the sauce and mushrooms over the chicken so it's covered. Refrigerate for up to 4 days.

3. **Prep for reheating:** Slice the thighs into strips right in the container so they heat evenly. Add 1 tbsp water and cover loosely with the lid set ajar or a damp paper towel.

4. **Microwave, first round:** Microwave at 50% power for 2 minutes. Stir the sauce and turn the chicken pieces.

5. **Microwave, second round:** Microwave at 50% power for 1-1½ minutes more, until the chicken is steaming throughout (74°C if you're checking). Let stand 1 minute, then stir. If the sauce looks greasy or separated, stir hard with a spoon or add another splash of water.

## Notes

Serve with a tray of roasted vegetables (broccoli, carrots, or Brussels sprouts, see `Air Fryer Brussels Sprouts.md`). Roast them while the chicken rests and the sauce comes together. For meal prep, keep the vegetables in a separate compartment so they do not soak in the sauce.
