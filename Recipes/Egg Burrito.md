# Egg Burrito

Crispy fried eggs with melted marble cheese and salsa wrapped in a large flour tortilla, sealed on the pan. A quick single-serving breakfast.

**Total Time:** 10 minutes
**Servings:** 1 burrito

## Ingredients

- 1 large flour tortilla (10-12 inch)
- 2 large eggs
- Pinch of salt
- 1 tbsp salsa
- Small amount of shredded marble cheese (~2 tbsp / 15g)
- Neutral oil for frying

## Instructions

1. Crack the eggs into a bowl, add the pinch of salt, and whisk until fully combined.

2. Heat a slick of oil in a nonstick pan until relatively hot — you want the eggs to sizzle on contact.

3. Pour in the eggs and fry without stirring, letting the underside crisp up. Once set, fold the first third of the egg in on itself.

4. While the eggs finish, shred your cheese.

5. Transfer the eggs onto the tortilla and top with the shredded cheese so it starts melting.

6. Add your tablespoon of salsa.

7. Fold in the sides and roll up tightly.

8. Place the burrito back on the hot pan seam-side down for 30-60 seconds to seal it up, then serve.
