# Maggi with Eggs

A packet of Maggi with two soft-boiled eggs dropped in whole and broken open at the table, so the yolk runs into the broth and thickens it. The eggs are the only real work, and they cook while you do nothing.

**Total Time:** 10 minutes
**Servings:** 1

## Ingredients

- 1 packet Maggi 2-Minute Noodles (70g), tastemaker included
- 2 large eggs
- ~1¼ cups water (less than the packet says)
- Chopped scallion or a shake of chili flakes, optional

## Instructions

1. Bring a small pot of water to a rolling boil. Lower the eggs in gently with a spoon — dropping them in cracks the shells against the bottom. Leave them 6 minutes, then take them out and set them aside. The yolk will still be liquid at the center, which is what you want.

2. Dump the egg water, or use a second pot. Bring ~1¼ cups of fresh water to a boil, break in the noodle block, and cook 2 minutes, turning the block over once it softens enough to move.

3. Stir in the tastemaker and pull the pot off the heat. Don't drain it — the starchy broth is the point, and using less water than the packet calls for keeps it from tasting thin.

4. Peel the eggs under a thin stream of cold running water, starting from the fat end where the air pocket is. The two minutes they spent sitting while the noodles cooked is enough to make them handleable.

5. Pour the noodles and all the broth into a bowl, halve the eggs, and set them on top cut side up. Scallions or chili flakes if you have them.

6. Break the yolks with your spoon and stir them through the broth right before you start eating — do it any earlier and they set into the hot liquid instead of staying silky.
