# Chicken Caesar Salad Meal Prep

The ATK Caesar dressing and croutons, with romaine and store-bought marinated chicken, packed for weeknight dinners.

**Prep Time:** 35 minutes
**Cook Time:** 30 minutes
**Servings:** 6

## Ingredients

### The Salad

- 3 romaine heads
- 0.8 cups Parmesan, grated

### The Chicken

- 3.45 lbs store-bought marinated chicken thighs (9 thighs, or three 3-thigh store packs; each serving uses 1.5 thighs, so one pack covers 2 servings)
- 1.5 tbsp vegetable oil

### The Croutons

- 4.5 oz baguette, cut into ¾-inch cubes
- 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 0.4 tsp pepper
- 0.3 tsp salt

### The Dressing

- 0.8 cups mayonnaise (¾ cup ≈ 5¼ black spoons)
- 0.5 cups Parmesan, grated
- 1.5 tbsp lemon juice
- 1.5 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1.5 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1.5 garlic cloves, minced
- 0.8 tsp salt
- 0.8 tsp pepper
- 6 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil

## Instructions

1. **Wash and dry the lettuce:** Pull off any wilted outer leaves from the romaine, separate the rest into leaves, and rinse under cold water. Shake each leaf over the sink, lay them in a single layer on a clean kitchen towel, cover with a second towel, and roll up loosely. Press gently, unroll, and leave the leaves out to air-dry while you do the next steps.

2. **Make the dressing:** In a bowl, whisk together the mayonnaise, Parmesan, lemon juice, Worcestershire, Dijon, garlic, salt, and pepper. Slowly whisk in the olive oil until thick. Taste it on a lettuce leaf rather than a spoon, and add a pinch more salt if it needs it. Transfer to a lidded bowl and refrigerate. The batch makes about 1½ cups, so plan on about 3 tbsp per dinner.

3. **Prep the chicken:** Lift the chicken out of the package, let the excess marinade drip off, and pat lightly with paper towels. Throw out the leftover marinade. If the breasts are thicker than about ¾ inch, slice them in half horizontally so the inside cooks before the outside burns.

4. **Pan-fry the chicken:** Heat the vegetable oil in a 12-inch skillet over medium heat until shimmering. Working in batches so the pan isn't crowded, cook the chicken until browned, 4–6 minutes. Flip and cook until the thickest part reads 165°F (74°C), 4–6 minutes more. Thighs are better at 175°F (80°C). Add a little more oil between batches if the pan looks dry. If the outside darkens before the inside is done, lower the heat and cover the pan.

5. **Rest and chill the chicken:** Move the chicken to a cutting board and rest 5 minutes. Slice it, spread it on a plate, and refrigerate until cold.

6. **Air-fry the croutons:** Toss the bread cubes with the olive oil, salt, and pepper. Air-fry at 350°F (175°C), shaking the basket halfway, until golden and dry all the way through, 6–8 minutes. Work in batches if the cubes don't fit in a single layer. Air fryers vary, so check at 5 minutes. Cool completely.

7. **Chop the lettuce:** Once the leaves are dry, cut them into 1-inch pieces. Blot any damp spots with a towel.

8. **Pack the dinners:** Line each container with a folded paper towel, then divide the lettuce and the salad Parmesan among them. Put the chicken (1.5 thighs per dinner) in a divided compartment or its own small container so you can pull it out to reheat. Cooked chicken keeps 3–4 days, so freeze any portions you won't eat by then and thaw them in the fridge the night before. Refrigerate the salads and dressing. Store the croutons at room temperature in small zip bags, one per dinner.

## Notes

Packaged lettuce: skip steps 1 and 7 and use about 1 kg (2¼ lb) of pre-washed chopped romaine. Blot it with a towel if it's damp.

Chicken flavor: lemon-herb, garlic, or plain grilled suit a Caesar. Skip teriyaki, BBQ, or sweet chili.

Breasts instead of thighs: use the same weight.

Dressing: no anchovies or vinegar, and half the original lemon, since bottled juice tastes harsher than fresh. If it still tastes sour, whisk in a bit more mayo, Parmesan, and a pinch of salt.

Short on olive oil: any neutral oil works in the dressing; vegetable oil or melted butter works for the croutons.
